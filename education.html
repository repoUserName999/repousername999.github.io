Certificate in Information Technology (Level 5) - Wintec

Feb 2025 - Current

In this course I am working on the following modules:

COMP501 Information Technology Operations

I am learning the assembly and disassembly of computers, troubleshooting common faults, and safe working practices with electrical equipment.

COMP502 Fundamentals of Programming and Problem Solving

I am gaining an introduction to programming with Python. This module is also developing my soft skills in critical thinking and problem solving. I am learning to debug and analyze code using efficient and logical methods.

COMP506 Interactive Digital Media

I am learning how to design and code websites using HTML and CSS. I am developing my team work skills as I work with other students to problem solve bugs and discover more efficient and user-friendly ways to design digital interfaces. Time management has been extremely important to success in this class, as assessments take a lot of extra work behind the scenes while also prioritising assignments from other modules.

INFO507 Fundamentals of Information Systems

I have been learning about the impact of IT on business and also gaining an introduction to database modelling. This has been the module that has helped me develop my team work skills the most, as many of our projects are completed in groups. Working with different personalities to accomplish a shared task in a given timeframe has been a really fulfilling challenge. We have also started learning the basics of Microsoft Access.

Certificate in Outdoor Experiences (Level 3) - NZDF

I achieved this qualification in NZDF basic training and it involved participating in a group outdoor activity and completing it safely.


The key components I accomplished for this qulaification were:
  • Planning for and carrying out a multi-day hike
  • The importance of Kaitiakitanga, our responsibility of guardianship and protection of the environment
  • Self-management
  • Teamwork and communication skills
  • Survival skills, including clothing layering, land navigation techniques, and safe river crossings
  • Personal resilience when facing fatigue, interpersonal conflicts and stressful environments
