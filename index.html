Hobbies

When I'm not at work, I love to spend time playing music, exploring the outdoors and doing some amateur photography.

Music

I have always loved music, my favourite genres being hard rock and metal. I learned keyboard from the age of 8 which gave me my music theory foundations, but I really became passionate about it when I took up drums at 13. This is my favourite instrument as well as guitar, which I began learning more recently.

I love the challenge of learning new songs and techniques, and this hobby is a great way to keep my mind busy when I am not studying or at work.

Outdoors

I am passionate about adventure and being in the outdoors, and I take any opportunity I can get to go hiking, mountain biking, kayaking or doing anything else that gets me into the fresh air.

In 2024 I crossed a long-time personal goal off my bucket-list when I climbed Mount Kilimanjaro, the highest free-standing mountain in the world. It was an incredible way to experience the culture of a country so different to anywhere else that I have been, and pushing through the altitude sickness to reach the summit in sub-zero temperatures was a rewarding challenge to overcome.
